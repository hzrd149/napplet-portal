| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-04-10 | Spoofing / Tampering | install review UI | high | mitigate | Render immutable server-derived facts, bind dialog to catalog ID, and invalidate/focus-return on replacement. |
